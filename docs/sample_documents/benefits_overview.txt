EMPLOYEE BENEFITS OVERVIEW
Effective Date: January 2024
Policy Owner: Human Resources

1. PURPOSE
This document summarises the benefits available to all permanent employees of Acme Corporation. Full details of each benefit are available in the Benefits Handbook on the intranet.

2. PENSION
Acme Corporation contributes 6% of basic salary to the company pension scheme. Employees contribute a minimum of 3%. The pension provider is Aviva. Employees may increase their contribution at any time via the HR self-service portal. New employees are auto-enrolled within 3 months of their start date.

3. PRIVATE HEALTH INSURANCE
All employees are eligible for private health insurance through Bupa from their first day of employment. Cover includes inpatient, day-patient, and outpatient treatment. Employees may add dependants at an additional cost through the flexible benefits portal.

4. LIFE ASSURANCE
Acme Corporation provides life assurance at 4x basic annual salary. Employees must nominate a beneficiary via the HR portal. This benefit is active from the first day of employment.

5. GYM SUBSIDY
Employees receive a monthly gym subsidy of up to £50 per month, reimbursed through payroll upon submission of receipts. Eligible facilities include any gym that is not operated by Acme Corporation.

6. CYCLE-TO-WORK SCHEME
The cycle-to-work scheme allows employees to purchase a bicycle and safety equipment tax-efficiently through salary sacrifice. Applications are processed twice per year (April and October). The maximum equipment value is £2,000.

7. FLEXIBLE BENEFITS
Through the flexible benefits portal, employees may: buy or sell up to 5 days of annual leave; increase life assurance cover; add dental and vision cover; access retail and travel discounts. The flexible benefits window opens each January.

8. EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME
The EAP provides free, confidential support for personal, financial, and work-related issues. Available 24/7 via phone (0800-ACME-EAP) and online portal. Services include counselling, legal advice, and financial guidance.
