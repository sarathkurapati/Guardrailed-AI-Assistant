IT INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCEDURE
Effective Date: February 2024
Policy Owner: IT Security

1. PURPOSE
This procedure defines how Acme Corporation identifies, reports, contains, and recovers from IT security incidents and service outages.

2. INCIDENT CATEGORIES
Incidents are classified by severity: P1 (Critical) — complete system outage or confirmed data breach; P2 (High) — significant system degradation or suspected breach; P3 (Medium) — limited service impact, no data loss; P4 (Low) — minor issue with workaround available.

3. REPORTING
All employees must report suspected IT incidents immediately to the IT Security team: email security@acmecorp.com or call the 24/7 hotline on +44 20 1234 5678. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate an incident independently. Do not discuss the incident on unsecured channels.

4. INCIDENT RESPONSE TEAM
The Incident Response Team (IRT) is activated for P1 and P2 incidents. The IRT includes the IT Security Lead, Head of IT Operations, Data Protection Officer (for data breaches), and a Communications lead. The CISO is notified for all P1 incidents within 15 minutes.

5. CONTAINMENT
Upon confirmation of an incident, the IRT will: isolate affected systems from the network; preserve evidence (logs, disk images); notify affected system owners; revoke compromised credentials. For data breaches, the DPO assesses whether ICO notification is required within 72 hours.

6. ERADICATION AND RECOVERY
After containment, the IRT will identify the root cause, remove malicious artefacts, patch vulnerabilities, and restore systems from verified backups. Recovery timelines for P1 incidents target a maximum of 4 hours to restore critical services.

7. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW
A post-incident review is conducted within 5 business days of resolution for P1 and P2 incidents. The review produces a root cause analysis and improvement actions. Findings are shared with senior leadership.

8. DOCUMENTATION
All incidents must be logged in the IT Service Management system with full timeline, actions taken, and resolution details. Records are retained for 5 years.
