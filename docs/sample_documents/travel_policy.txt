BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY
Effective Date: January 2024
Policy Owner: Finance Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy provides guidance for employees who travel for business purposes, covering booking procedures, permitted expenditure, and safety requirements.

2. APPROVAL
All business travel must be approved by the employee's line manager before booking. Travel exceeding £1,000 in total cost requires Finance Director approval. International travel requires approval from the relevant VP at least 5 working days before departure.

3. BOOKING
All travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel management system (TravelPerk). Direct bookings are only permitted where TravelPerk does not offer a suitable option, in which case receipts must be submitted for reimbursement. The corporate travel team can be reached at travel@acmecorp.com.

4. AIR TRAVEL
Economy class is mandatory for all flights under 6 hours. Business class may be approved by the relevant VP for flights over 6 hours. Employees must book the lowest available fare that meets business requirements. Personal loyalty points or miles should not influence flight selection.

5. RAIL TRAVEL
Standard class is the default. First class is permissible for journeys over 3 hours when the ticket is booked at least 7 days in advance at a reasonable price (under 150% of the standard fare). Advance purchase tickets should be used wherever possible.

6. ACCOMMODATION
Employees should use preferred hotels where available (listed on the intranet). The hotel rate cap is £150/night in the UK and £200/night internationally. Exceptions require Finance Director approval.

7. TRAVEL SAFETY
Employees travelling to regions rated High Risk by the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) must obtain approval from the Head of Legal and complete a travel safety briefing. Travel insurance is provided automatically for all TravelPerk bookings.

8. SUSTAINABLE TRAVEL
Employees are encouraged to choose rail over air for journeys under 3 hours and to combine multiple business objectives into a single trip where possible. Acme Corporation tracks its business travel carbon footprint and reports on it annually.
