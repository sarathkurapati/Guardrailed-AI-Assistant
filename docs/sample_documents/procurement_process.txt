PROCUREMENT PROCESS
Effective Date: January 2024
Policy Owner: Finance Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy describes the process for purchasing goods and services on behalf of Acme Corporation, ensuring value for money, compliance, and appropriate authorisation.

2. PURCHASING THRESHOLDS AND APPROVAL LEVELS
Purchases under £500: may be approved by the employee's direct line manager. Purchases £500–£5,000: require Finance Business Partner approval. Purchases £5,001–£25,000: require Head of Department and Finance Director approval. Purchases over £25,000: require CFO and CEO approval. All technology purchases regardless of value require IT Security sign-off.

3. SUPPLIER SELECTION
For purchases over £5,000, at least three competitive quotes must be obtained and documented. Existing preferred suppliers (listed on the intranet) may be used without additional quotes for purchases under £25,000. New suppliers must complete the Supplier Due Diligence form before any purchase order is raised.

4. PURCHASE ORDERS
A Purchase Order (PO) must be raised in the financial system (NetSuite) before any goods or services are ordered. Invoices received without a corresponding PO will not be processed for payment. Emergency purchases without a PO require retrospective approval within 48 hours.

5. CONTRACT REVIEW
All contracts with a value over £10,000 or a term exceeding 12 months must be reviewed by Legal before signing. Employees must not sign contracts on behalf of the company without written authorisation to do so.

6. ASSET MANAGEMENT
All assets purchased over £500 must be tagged and registered in the asset management system. IT assets are managed by the IT department. Physical assets are managed by Facilities.

7. SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT
Acme Corporation is committed to responsible procurement. Suppliers are assessed on environmental and social governance criteria as part of the due diligence process. Sustainable alternatives should be considered for all purchases where cost-competitive.

8. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Purchases made outside this policy without appropriate approval may result in personal liability for the employee and disciplinary action.
