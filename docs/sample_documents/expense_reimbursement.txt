EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Policy Owner: Finance Department

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by employees in the performance of their duties.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Eligible expenses include: business travel (rail, air, taxi); accommodation for overnight business trips; client entertainment (subject to limits); business meals with colleagues or clients; approved professional development courses and materials; home office equipment (subject to prior approval).

3. EXPENSE LIMITS
Meals (per person): Lunch £20, Dinner £40. Client entertainment: £75 per head with manager pre-approval; receipts required. Hotel accommodation: up to £150/night in the UK, £200/night internationally. Air travel must be economy class for journeys under 6 hours and business class only with Director-level approval for longer routes.

4. SUBMISSION PROCESS
Expenses must be submitted via the Concur expense system within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed unless exceptional circumstances apply. All claims require original receipts or electronic equivalents. Claims must be approved by the employee's line manager before processing.

5. PAYMENT TIMELINE
Approved expense claims are processed in the monthly payroll run. Claims approved before the 15th of the month will be paid at month-end. Claims approved after the 15th will be paid at the following month-end.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
The following are not reimbursable: personal entertainment; alcohol consumed without a business purpose; parking fines or traffic penalties; personal travel upgrades; expenses claimed more than 60 days after incurrence without justification.

7. CORPORATE CARD
Employees who regularly incur business expenses may apply for a company corporate card through Finance. Corporate card expenditure is subject to the same policy limits and approval requirements.

8. FRAUD
Submission of false expense claims constitutes fraud and will result in immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination and referral to law enforcement.
