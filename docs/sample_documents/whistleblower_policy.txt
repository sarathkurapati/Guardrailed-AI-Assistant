WHISTLEBLOWER POLICY (SPEAK UP POLICY)
Effective Date: January 2024
Policy Owner: Legal & Compliance

1. PURPOSE
Acme Corporation is committed to operating with integrity. This policy encourages employees to report concerns about wrongdoing without fear of retaliation, in compliance with the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998.

2. WHAT TO REPORT
Employees should report concerns about: fraud, financial irregularities, or theft; breaches of legal or regulatory requirements; health and safety risks; environmental violations; cover-up of any of the above; breaches of the Code of Conduct. This policy is not intended for routine HR grievances (which should use the Grievance Procedure).

3. HOW TO REPORT
Concerns can be raised via: the anonymous Whistleblower Hotline: 0800-ACME-ETH (available 24/7, operated by an independent third party); the online portal: speakup.acmecorp.com; directly to the Chief Compliance Officer: compliance@acmecorp.com; or to the Chair of the Audit Committee for concerns involving senior management.

4. ANONYMITY
Employees may report concerns anonymously. Anonymous reports will be investigated to the extent possible. Employees who identify themselves will be kept informed of the outcome where legally permissible.

5. PROTECTION FROM RETALIATION
Any employee who reports a concern in good faith will be protected from retaliation, victimisation, or detriment. This includes protection against dismissal, demotion, disciplinary action, or exclusion. Retaliation against a whistleblower is itself a disciplinary matter and may result in termination.

6. INVESTIGATION PROCESS
All reports are triaged by the Chief Compliance Officer or a designated independent investigator. The investigation will be proportionate to the nature of the concern. The reporting employee will be notified of the outcome within 30 days where possible.

7. FALSE REPORTS
This policy protects employees who raise concerns in good faith, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. However, deliberately false or malicious reports will be treated as a disciplinary matter.

8. RECORD KEEPING
All reports and investigation outcomes are recorded confidentially and retained for 7 years.
