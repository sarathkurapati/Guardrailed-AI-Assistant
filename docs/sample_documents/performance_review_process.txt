PERFORMANCE REVIEW PROCESS
Effective Date: January 2024
Policy Owner: Human Resources

1. PURPOSE
This document describes the performance review cycle at Acme Corporation, the criteria used, and the process for setting objectives and providing feedback.

2. REVIEW CYCLE
Acme Corporation operates a bi-annual performance review cycle. Mid-year reviews take place in June and annual reviews in December. In addition, quarterly check-ins between employee and line manager are mandatory and should be documented in the HR system.

3. OBJECTIVES AND GOAL SETTING
At the start of each year (and following a mid-year review), employees and managers agree on 3–5 SMART objectives. Objectives must align with team and company OKRs. Goals are recorded in the HR performance module and reviewed at each check-in.

4. RATING SCALE
Performance is rated on a five-point scale: 1 - Does Not Meet Expectations; 2 - Partially Meets Expectations; 3 - Meets Expectations; 4 - Exceeds Expectations; 5 - Outstanding. A rating of 1 or 2 triggers a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

5. 360-DEGREE FEEDBACK
The annual review includes 360-degree feedback from peers and stakeholders. Employees nominate up to 5 reviewers; the line manager may add or remove nominees. Feedback is anonymised before being shared with the employee.

6. CALIBRATION
Before finalising ratings, managers participate in a calibration session with their peers and HR Business Partner to ensure consistency across the team and department.

7. COMPENSATION LINK
Performance ratings influence annual salary review decisions. Employees rated 4 or 5 are eligible for performance bonuses as defined in the Reward Policy. Employees on a PIP are not eligible for pay increases or bonuses during the plan period.

8. APPEALS
Employees who disagree with their performance rating may submit a written appeal to HR within 10 working days of receiving the rating. Appeals are reviewed by the HR Director and a panel independent of the rating manager.
