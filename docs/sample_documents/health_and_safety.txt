HEALTH AND SAFETY POLICY
Effective Date: January 2024
Policy Owner: Facilities & HR

1. PURPOSE
Acme Corporation is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors, in compliance with the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974.

2. RESPONSIBILITIES
Senior Leadership is responsible for ensuring adequate resources are allocated to health and safety. Line Managers are responsible for conducting risk assessments for their teams and ensuring compliance within their areas. All employees are responsible for following H&S procedures and reporting hazards promptly.

3. RISK ASSESSMENTS
Risk assessments are conducted for all work activities that may pose a hazard. Assessments are reviewed annually or following any incident. Employees returning from extended sick leave undergo a back-to-work risk assessment.

4. FIRE SAFETY
Fire evacuation procedures are posted at all exits. Fire drills are conducted twice per year. Employees must familiarise themselves with the nearest fire exit on their first day. Fire marshals are designated on each floor and are identified by their orange lanyards.

5. FIRST AID
First aiders are identified on the Facilities intranet page and on floor plans. First aid kits are located in all kitchen areas and reception. If you require first aid, call the Facilities team on extension 4600.

6. DSE AND ERGONOMICS
All employees using display screen equipment for more than 1 hour per day are entitled to a DSE assessment. Assessments are conducted by the Facilities team or via the online self-assessment tool on the intranet. Employees may request ergonomic equipment (e.g. standing desks, specialist keyboards) through the IT service desk.

7. MENTAL HEALTH
Acme Corporation recognises mental health as an integral part of overall wellbeing. Mental health first aiders are available in each office. The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) provides free, confidential counselling: 0800-ACME-EAP.

8. ACCIDENT REPORTING
All accidents, near-misses, and dangerous occurrences must be reported to Facilities immediately and logged in the Accident Book (available at reception). RIDDOR-reportable incidents will be reported to the HSE within the required timescales.
