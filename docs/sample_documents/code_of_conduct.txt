CODE OF CONDUCT
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Policy Owner: Legal & Compliance

1. PURPOSE
Acme Corporation is committed to maintaining a workplace that is professional, respectful, and free from harassment and discrimination. This Code of Conduct applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors.

2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOUR
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with dignity and respect. Aggressive, abusive, or demeaning behaviour — whether verbal, written, or physical — is strictly prohibited. This includes behaviour in physical offices, digital communication channels, and company-sponsored events.

3. HARASSMENT AND DISCRIMINATION
Acme Corporation prohibits harassment and discrimination based on age, gender, race, religion, nationality, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Harassment includes unwelcome conduct that creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment. Any employee found to have engaged in harassment or discrimination will face disciplinary action up to and including termination.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or professional relationships that could create a conflict of interest with their duties at Acme Corporation. Undisclosed conflicts of interest are a disciplinary matter. Employees should not make business decisions from which they personally benefit.

5. GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
Employees must not accept gifts, hospitality, or any benefit from a supplier, client, or competitor that exceeds £50 in value without prior written approval from their line manager and Compliance. Giving gifts to clients or partners must comply with the Anti-Bribery Policy.

6. USE OF COMPANY RESOURCES
Company resources — including IT systems, email, equipment, and premises — are for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable provided it does not interfere with job duties or breach security policies.

7. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
Employees who witness or experience a violation of this Code should report it to their line manager, HR, or via the anonymous Whistleblower Hotline (0800-ACME-ETH). Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

8. DISCIPLINARY CONSEQUENCES
Violations of this Code may result in verbal or written warnings, suspension, or termination of employment depending on the severity and frequency of the breach.
